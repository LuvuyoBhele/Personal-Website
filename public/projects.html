Tracing the Cosmic Web: Unveiling the Universe's Hidden Structure

caption: picture of the cosmic web

This article explores the mystery of the universe's missing baryonic matter and how scientists trace it through the cosmic web-a vast network of filaments made of dark matter, gas, and galaxies. It highlights how computational tools like Python and the Friends-of-Friends algorithm can simulate these hidden structures, making the invisible visible. Blending scientific insight with personal reflection, the piece emphasizes the power of data and design to reveal meaning, inspire curiosity, and build a legacy of understanding across both cosmic and human scales.
